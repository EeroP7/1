# Lumora 🌿

**A complete, launch-ready digital-products e-commerce business.**
Premium digital planners & life systems (Notion · printable · iPad) for people
who want a calmer, more intentional life.

This folder contains everything: the product, the website, and the executed
business plan — built from market research up.

> **Why digital products?** ~92% gross margin, zero inventory, instant global
> fulfilment. It's the leanest credible path to $100k/month, and it's the one
> category where the actual sellable product could be authored here, now.

## What's in here

```
lumora/
├── store/        → the storefront (Next.js 14 + Tailwind). Builds, runs, verified.
├── product/      → the ACTUAL products. The Daily Planner is a finished PDF.
└── business/     → business plan, financial model (code+CSV), marketing + assets.
```

## The plan, in one paragraph

Sell a connected family of digital planners with a price ladder ($19–$97)
engineered for a **~$42 average order value** at ~92% margin. Drive traffic with
a compounding engine — Pinterest/TikTok/IG organic, an owned email list, SEO
content, then paid ads kept at **CAC ≤ $15** (LTV:CAC 3:1) — to reach **~79
profitable orders/day**. The model (`business/financial_model.py`) crosses
**$100,000/month in Month 9** and exits Year 1 at a ~$1.46M/yr run-rate.
