# Lumora — Marketing Plan

Margins are ~92%, so the entire job is **distribution**: get ~91,600 qualified
sessions/month converting at ~2.6% to hit $100k/month. Here's the engine.

## The funnel

```
Discover (organic social, SEO, ads)
   → Capture (free printable weekly page → email list)
      → Nurture (welcome flow, Journal, social)
         → Convert (flagship offer, bundles, order bump, upsell)
            → Repeat & refer (new packs, email, affiliate/UGC)
```

## Channel playbooks

### 1. Pinterest (top discovery channel for planners)
- Why: evergreen, search-driven, planner/aesthetic content overperforms, long
  pin lifespan (months vs. hours).
- Do: 3–5 fresh pins/day; every product + every Journal post becomes multiple
  pins; keyword-rich titles ("2026 digital planner for Notion"); link pins to
  product and lead-magnet pages.
- Goal: the cheapest qualified traffic in the mix; compounds for free.

### 2. TikTok + Instagram Reels
- Why: where aesthetic productivity content goes viral; near-zero CAC when it hits.
- Do: daily short video — "plan with me", Notion setup tours, "5 planners I tried
  and the only one I kept", aesthetic flat-lays, before/after of a chaotic week.
  Hook in the first 1.5s; CTA to "free planner in bio".
- Repurpose every video across both platforms + Pinterest Idea Pins.

### 3. Email (highest-converting, fully owned)
- Lead magnet: the free printable weekly page (already on-site) → 10% code.
- Flows: Welcome (5 emails, written — see `launch-assets/email-welcome-sequence.md`),
  abandoned checkout, post-purchase upsell, win-back.
- Cadence: 1–2 broadcasts/week (a Journal tip + a soft offer). Owned list is the
  single biggest lever on LTV and on launch revenue.

### 4. Paid acquisition (Meta + Pinterest + TikTok ads)
- Turn on only after 2–3 organic creatives prove demand. Scale winners.
- Structure: broad targeting, let the algorithm find buyers; test 5+ creatives/wk;
  kill anything with CAC > $18, scale anything < $12.
- Guardrail: blended CAC ≤ $15 (keeps LTV:CAC ≥ 3:1). Budget grows only as ROAS holds.

### 5. SEO / content (compounding, free)
- The Journal targets high-intent terms. Publish 1–2 posts/week; each ends with a
  product CTA. 3 cornerstone posts are already written.
- Targets: "best digital planner 2026", "notion planner template", "how to stick
  to a planner", "goodnotes planner", "sunday reset routine".

### 6. Affiliate / UGC creators
- Recruit micro-creators in productivity/wellness; 20–30% commission per sale via
  a link/code. Their content doubles as ads and social proof. Low risk
  (pay-per-sale), high leverage.

## Conversion-rate levers (cheaper than more traffic)
- Hero offer clarity + savings framing (Life System "$97, was $189").
- Order bump at checkout (+$10 add-on) and one post-purchase upsell → lifts AOV
  ~15–25% with zero extra traffic.
- Real reviews + UGC on product pages (replace placeholders ASAP).
- Urgency on launches (discount windows), not fake scarcity.
- Fast, mobile-first site (already built) + Apple/Google Pay via Stripe.

## Calendar
- **Always-on:** daily social, weekly email + blog, daily Pinterest.
- **Monthly:** one themed launch/promo (new pack, bundle, seasonal).
- **Seasonal spikes:** New Year (planning season — biggest), Q4 gifting,
  back-to-school (Aug–Sep), Mother's Day.

## KPIs (review weekly)
- Sessions, conversion rate, AOV, orders, revenue.
- Blended + per-channel CAC, ROAS, email list size & revenue/email.
- Refund rate (keep < 4%), repeat-purchase rate.
- North-star: **profitable orders/day** trending to 79/day ($100k/mo).
