# Your 7-Day Setup Plan

Welcome to Lumora. Don't try to use everything at once — that's exactly why
planners get abandoned. Follow this gentle on-ramp and the system will still be
with you in a year.

**Day 1 — Just today.** Open the Daily page (Notion) or print one daily sheet.
Write your three priorities for today. That's it.

**Day 2 — Repeat.** Do the three-priority ritual again. Notice how it feels to
finish a short list instead of failing a long one.

**Day 3 — Add a habit.** Open the Habit tracker. Choose *one* habit to track.
Use the habit stack: "After I [existing habit], I will [new habit]."

**Day 4 — Plan tomorrow tonight.** Spend two minutes setting tomorrow's three
priorities before bed. Mornings get dramatically calmer.

**Day 5 — Weekly view.** Set three priorities for the rest of the week. Make
sure your daily threes ladder up to them.

**Day 6 — One more area.** Add whichever pack speaks to you right now — wellness,
finance, or meals. Just one.

**Day 7 — The Sunday reset.** Run the 5-step weekly reset (brain dump → review →
weekly priorities → meals & calendar → small self-care). You're now running the
full Lumora rhythm.

From here: keep the daily three-priority habit as your anchor. Everything else is
optional support. Calm beats complete.
