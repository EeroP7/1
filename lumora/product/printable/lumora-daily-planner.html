The 3-Priority Method

Most planners fail because they ask too much. Lumora asks for three things. Each day, choose the three priorities that — if completed — would make today a win. Three is small enough to finish, and finishing is what brings you back tomorrow.

01

Choose three

Each morning, write your three priorities. Everything else goes on the "later" list.

02

Protect your time

Block time for priority one before the day fills up with everyone else's requests.

03

Close the day

Tick what's done, note one win, and carry the rest forward without guilt.

The weekly rhythm

Use the Weekly Plan each Sunday to set three weekly priorities. Each day's three priorities should ladder up to them. Once a month, fill the calendar and habit tracker. That's the whole system. Print what you need, as often as you need it.
