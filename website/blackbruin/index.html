Technology

Sovereign power.
Zero gears.

The robust mechanical construction combined with serviceable torque-transmitting components transmits hydraulic energy directly to the shaft — high torque even at the lowest speeds, with nothing in between to wear out.

01

Robust piston design

Radial pistons on a cam ring produce continuous high power efficiently — a construction proven over six decades in the field.

02

Several output shafts

Internal or external splines, solid or the uniquely useful through-hole shaft — matched to your machine, not the other way around.

03

Shift-on-the-go 2-speed

Switch between 100% and 50% displacement under load — torque for the bite, speed for the cycle, no stopping.

04

Triple-sealed protection

Sealed against dust, slurry and seawater. A Black Bruin goes on after any other solution gives up.

Heritage

The bear from
Jyväskylä.

Black Bruin radial piston motors are based on a Finnish invention — innovator Ilmari Louhio's original idea of a vehicle that could go anywhere and haul loads from the remotest work sites. Over 65 years later, the same radial piston cam ring technology drives machinery on every continent.

A Black Bruin goes on after any other solution gives up. Its serviceability is superior to any other solution available — torque-transmitting components can be serviced without removing the motor from the machine.
